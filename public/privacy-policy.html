1. Scope and Application

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and practices of Credence Fleet Management System (“Credence FMS”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) regarding the collection, processing, storage, and transfer of personal, vehicular, and operational data through our web platform and related backend endpoints.

This policy applies to all registered enterprise clients, logistics coordinators, supervisors, drivers, transport operators, warehouse personnel, and visitors who access or interact with any section of the platform.

2. Information We Collect

Credence FMS collects only data strictly required to deliver vehicle dispatching, maintenance scheduling, logistics tracking, and regulatory audit compliance.

Primary information streams

  • Account and credential details: Full name, corporate username, encrypted authentication hashes, contact email, phone number, and worker role permissions.
  • Vehicle asset telematics: Vehicle registration number, Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), engine runtime hours, odometer readings, fuel consumption levels, and tyre wear metrics.
  • Trip and geolocation telemetry: Live GPS coordinates recorded exclusively during active trip assignments, route departure and arrival timestamps, rail/road goods receipts, and intermediate checkpost validations.
  • Maintenance and inspection documentation: Pre-trip vehicle inspection forms, service invoices, roadside assistance call logs, and incident reports.
  • System security metadata: Browser type, operating system version, session tokens (crdnsMaintToken), IP address, and authenticated API transaction logs.

3. How Data Is Used

All data recorded in Credence FMS is processed under legitimate commercial and regulatory interests. We do not engage in consumer data profiling or targeted behavioral advertising.

  • Dispatch coordination, trip routing, and logistical scheduling across regional hubs.
  • Preventative maintenance monitoring to detect vehicular component failure before roadside breakdown.
  • Driver duty-of-care verification and adherence to statutory driving-hour regulations.
  • Accurate verification of fuel allocations, tyre inventory, and maintenance expense claims.
  • Enforcement of role-based authorization ensuring sensitive records are accessible solely to permitted supervisors and administrators.

4. Retention Ledger

In compliance with commercial transport laws and standard audit guidelines, records are retained according to the following retention schedule:

Data Category Primary Records Retention Period Scheduled Disposal
Active Telematics GPS coordinates, speed, route points 12 months Aggregated or purged annually
Trip & Consignment Daily trip sheets, goods receipts (GR) 3 years Permanent cryptographic archival
Maintenance History Part replacements, tyre logs, inspections Asset lifecycle Retained until vehicle decommission
Financial & Expense Fuel bills, driver expense slips, LR notes 7 years Statutory tax and audit expiration
User Accounts Worker profiles, contact data, auth logs Duration of employment De-identified 90 days after departure

5. Security and Access Control

Credence FMS implements defense-in-depth security architectures to ensure enterprise fleet records remain intact, confidential, and resilient against unauthorized interference.

  • Encryption standards: All network communication is mandated through TLS 1.3 encryption. Data stored at rest is secured using AES-256 standards.
  • Granular role-based access: Access permissions are segmented between Superadmin, Supervisor, and Worker roles, strictly restricting visibility to authorized entities.
  • Token management: Session keys are distributed using authenticated tokens and secured cookie attributes, eliminating plaintext password persistence.
  • Tamper-evident audit logging: All modifications to vehicle documents, driver permissions, and financial approvals record timestamps and originating operator IDs.

6. Third-Party Integrations

We partner with dedicated third-party infrastructure providers to enable core application services. Each service provider is bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements:

  • Geospatial services: Mapping and tile rendering (Google Maps API, Leaflet OpenStreetMap) to display visual routes and vehicle locations.
  • Real-time communication: WebSocket protocols for live dashboard notifications, alert broadcasts, and active fleet status changes.
  • Document generation utilities: Client-side PDF and Excel export libraries executing securely in the user browser without transmitting export content to third-party endpoints.

Credence FMS never sells, monetizes, or leases client fleet records or driver telemetry to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction and operational status, you possess defined rights regarding your stored personal and professional data:

  • Right of access: You may request an export of your personal profile, logged trips, and vehicle assignments.
  • Right of rectification: You may request immediate correction of inaccurate contact information, license records, or identity documents.
  • Right to restriction: In cases of disputed records, you may request processing restrictions pending administrative review.
  • Right of erasure: You may request deactivation and deletion of personal profile data, subject to mandatory transport retention laws.
